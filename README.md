# E-Commerce-Sales-Operation-Analyticss
A unified Sales &amp; Operations Performance Dashboard that combines sales analytics, operational efficiency, and customer satisfaction metrics. Built with Power BI/Tableau, SQL, and Python, this project empowers teams to make data-driven decisions with clear KPIs, seller insights, and regional performance mapping.

The ECommerce Sales & Operations Performance Dashboard unifies sales analytics, operational efficiency, and customer satisfaction into one streamlined interface. It helps teams make data-driven decisions by visualizing KPIs across revenue, orders, logistics, and customer feedback.

🔑 Features
Revenue Tracking → Monitor revenue across states and categories

Order Management → Track total orders and delayed orders with warning icons

Seller Insights → Benchmark top sellers and audit worst performers

Regional Performance → Map delivery efficiency across regions

Customer Satisfaction → Review scores and sentiment analysis

📂 Dashboard Pages
Dashboard Overview → Financial health, savings, investments, profit margin
Sales Analytics → Orders, revenue by category, customer retention
Operations & Satisfaction → Delivery days, delayed orders, review scores
Executive Summary → Condensed KPIs, seller benchmarking, regional insights
🧠 Business Insights
🚚 Streamline logistics in high-delay regions
🏆 Reward top-performing sellers
😊 Improve customer experience through faster delivery
📊 Drive strategy with actionable data


AI-driven customer sentiment classification
